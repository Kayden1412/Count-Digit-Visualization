How many decimal digits?

Algorithm 1 estimates log10(v) from the bit length, then fixes the answer with one comparison. No floating point. No loops. Exact for every unsigned 64-bit integer.

digits = 1 + ⌊(63 − clz(v)) × 77 / 256⌋ + [v ≥ 10a+1]
